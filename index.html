How it works

Four things to understand before exploring a HELOC

Understanding your equity, credit profile and the lender’s actual terms can help you compare HELOC options more carefully.

1

Check your equity

Use our free calculator to find out how much equity your home currently holds.

2

Review your credit

Review your credit reports and history. Lenders set their own credit requirements, so there is no single universal HELOC score threshold.

3

Compare lenders

Compare the APR, index and margin, fees, draw and repayment terms, minimum-payment rules and other conditions.

4

Read the terms

If you apply, review the lender’s disclosures carefully so you understand the credit limit, access rules, payments, fees and repayment terms.
