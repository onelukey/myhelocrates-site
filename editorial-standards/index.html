Editorial Standards & Methodology

How topics get chosen, how published content is verified, and what happens if something's wrong.

MyHelocRates.com exists to help US homeowners understand Home Equity Lines of Credit — a financial product that is widely available but poorly explained. This page sets out, plainly, how the content on this site gets made: how topics are picked, where the numbers come from, how often they're revisited, and what to do if you spot an error.

Editorial independence: MyHelocRates.com has no affiliate relationships, lender partnerships, or paid placements. This site is funded by display advertising only. No lender or financial company has ever paid for or influenced any content, ranking, or recommendation on this site.

How topics are chosen

Topics are chosen based on the real questions US homeowners actually have about HELOCs — drawn from search behaviour, common points of confusion identified in CFPB consumer complaint data, and gaps noticed in existing coverage elsewhere. Priority goes to topics where clear, honest information is genuinely hard to find, rather than topics that are easy to monetise.

How published content is verified

MyHelocRates.com prioritises primary and authoritative sources when researching financial topics, particularly figures, interest rates, lending rules and regulatory information. AI-assisted tools may help organise research and prepare drafts, but they are not treated as authoritative sources. Articles are reviewed by the publisher for clarity, relevance and usefulness, and external source links are checked to confirm they lead to the intended resources. Not every financial claim has undergone independent verification against its original source. The table below shows the source categories we rely on most and what each is used for.

Where the information comes from

Federal ReserveH.15 release (Prime Rate), FOMC calendar, G.19 Consumer Credit data
CFPBConsumer protection guidance, complaint data, regulatory context
IRSPublication 936 and official guidance for all tax-deductibility content
Lender rate surveysBankrate, Curinos, and LendingTree published national averages
Industry reportsZonda/Remodeling Magazine Cost vs. Value Report for renovation ROI data
Individual lendersPublished rate pages and terms, verified directly on the lender's own site

Financial figures and statistics should be checked against authoritative sources before publication, particularly where they could influence a reader's understanding of borrowing costs, eligibility or regulatory requirements. AI-generated estimates are not considered verified financial facts. Where a figure cannot be reliably supported, our editorial aim is to verify, qualify or remove it. Not every historical claim has undergone independent fact-checking, and we continue to review and improve published material. See the section below for more information about how AI assists with research and drafting.

How often content is reviewed and updated

MyHelocRates.com reviews its educational content as part of periodic website audits, focusing on accuracy, clarity, source reliability and continued usefulness to readers. Particular attention is given to financial figures, regulatory information, outdated references and broken links. Where possible, our articles explain lasting financial principles and direct readers to authoritative sources for information that may change over time. Content may also be reviewed when an error is reported or a significant issue is identified. Review dates are updated when a page has undergone a substantive editorial review, rather than automatically or simply because time has passed.

Corrections policy

Found something wrong?

If you spot a factual error, an outdated figure, or a broken source link anywhere on this site, please get in touch using "Content correction" as the topic. Every correction request is reviewed personally by Mike. Confirmed errors are corrected as soon as practical, and for significant factual corrections the update date on the affected page is revised too.

Who writes this content

MyHelocRates.com is independently published and edited by Mike Lucas, the site's founder. Mike uses AI-assisted tools to help identify potential gaps in educational coverage, organise research, develop drafts and improve explanations. He reviews published material for clarity, relevance and usefulness, and checks external links to confirm they lead to the intended resources. AI-generated material is not treated as an authoritative financial source, and not every financial claim has undergone independent fact-checking. Mike retains editorial responsibility for the content published on the site. You can read more about the site's purpose and Mike's background on the About page.

Reminder: Nothing on this site constitutes financial, tax, or legal advice. Content is for general education. Always consult a qualified professional before making borrowing decisions. See the full Disclaimer.
